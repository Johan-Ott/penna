Privacy policy

In short: Penna collects nothing about you, and your texts stay with you.

Your books

Your books are saved as files on your device, in a folder you choose. Penna sends them nowhere. If you sign in with Google, a copy of your books, your profile and your settings is kept in the Penna folder in your own Drive, so that they follow you to your other devices. Penna can then only see the files Penna itself created there. Penna has no server of its own.

No accounts, no tracking

Penna has no accounts of its own, no ads and no statistics about how you use the app. Your texts are never used to train AI and never sold.

Services Penna contacts

  • GitHub, to check for a new version.
  • jsDelivr, to fetch dictionaries for the spell checker.
  • Google, only if you sign in with Google.
  • Formspree, only if you send feedback from the app.

These services see that a device is asking, such as its IP address, but never what you write. Feedback holds what you write in the form yourself.

Error reports

Errors are only kept in the app. An error report is never sent by itself; you copy it and paste it yourself when you ask for help.

Feedback

If you send feedback from the app, it goes through Formspree, which stores the message in the United States. It holds what you write, your email if you give it, Penna's version and, if you choose, an error report without any text from your books. Feedback is kept as long as it is needed to answer and improve Penna, and deleted if you ask.

The website

The website has no cookies, no statistics and loads nothing from others. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps logs with IP addresses to protect the service.

Who is responsible, and your rights

Penna is made by Lone Lodge, a one-person studio in Sweden, which is the data controller for the little that is sent here: feedback and email. You have the right to know what is held about you, to have it corrected and to have it deleted. Write to the address below. If you are not satisfied, you can turn to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection, imy.se.
