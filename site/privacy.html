Privacy policy

In short: Skrivro collects nothing about you, and your texts stay with you.

Your books

Your books are saved as files on your device, in a folder you choose. Skrivro sends them nowhere. If you sign in with Google, a copy of your books, your profile and your settings is kept in the Skrivro folder in your own Drive, so that they follow you to your other devices. Skrivro can then only see the files Skrivro itself created there. Skrivro has no server of its own.

Skrivro's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It is used only for syncing.

No accounts, no tracking

Skrivro has no accounts of its own, no ads and no statistics about how you use the app. Your texts are never used to train AI and never sold.

Services Skrivro contacts

  • GitHub, to check for a new version.
  • jsDelivr, to fetch dictionaries for the spell checker.
  • Google, only if you sign in with Google.

These services see that a device is asking, such as its IP address, but never what you write. Feedback holds what you write in the form yourself.

Delete your data

Skrivro has no server, so everything is with you. To delete it: remove your books in Skrivro or their folder on your device. If you signed in with Google, also delete the Skrivro folder in your Google Drive, and remove Skrivro's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Uninstalling the app also removes its settings on the device. Nothing is kept anywhere else.

Error reports

The latest errors are kept only on your device, so that they remain if Skrivro crashes. An error report is never sent by itself: after a crash Skrivro asks whether you want to email it to us, and you can also copy it yourself when you ask for help.

Feedback

Feedback from the app is an ordinary email sent by your own mail program, and you see everything before it goes: what you write, Skrivro's version and, if you choose, an error report without any text from your books. The email lands in my Gmail. I keep it as long as it is needed to answer and improve Skrivro, and delete it if you ask.

The website

The website has no cookies, no statistics and loads nothing from others. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps logs with IP addresses to protect the service.
