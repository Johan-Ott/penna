Where we are

Penna is in open beta, free for everyone. The latest version is 0.4, for Windows and Android. We keep building all the time, with what you write to us as the map.

New in 0.4

  • A bookshelf with all your books, and an example book to try things out with.
  • You choose your language the first time you start Penna, and only the dictionaries you need are downloaded. The app is small from the start.
  • A guide that shows you around the app, one step at a time, and can be turned on again under Help.
  • Send feedback straight from the app, as an ordinary email you see before it goes.
  • Faster writing in long chapters, on the phone too.
