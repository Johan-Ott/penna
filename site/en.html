The book and its notes

Everything about the book, where you write it.

Parts, chapters and scenes in a tree you rearrange with a drag. Beside them are people, places and things, and their names find their own way into the text.

  • Word counts for every scene, chapter and part
  • A person shows their lines and where they are named
  • A series shares its notes between the books

Review

A second reader who never tires.

Review reads along with you and points out what you stop seeing yourself after the third read-through.

  • Repetitions within a few sentences
  • Narrative voice, pacing and what you promised the reader
  • Suggestions you accept or reject one at a time

Read as a book

See it as a book, long before it is one.

One press, and the text is set in spreads with drop caps and page numbers. Correct it right on the page, and the correction is in the manuscript.

  • Turn the pages with the arrows, like a real book
  • Themes and typefaces for how the book should look

Notes and goals

Room for your thoughts beside the text.

Sticky notes to write and draw on, in five colours, that stay where you put them. And reference pictures for the scene you are writing.

  • A pen and an eraser on every note
  • Goals for the day, the scene, the chapter and the book
  • Writing sprints and an overview of how the book grows

Publish

From first draft to finished book.

When it is done, Penna makes a standard manuscript for publishers, an e-book, a print PDF and a cover. You can also get the book as a folder of plain files.

  • A preview of the pages while you choose
  • Synopsis and cover letter, and where you have sent it

Phone

In your pocket.

The same book on your phone, for a scene on the bus or an idea before you fall asleep. With Google Drive the text follows you between devices, and Penna only sees its own files there.

Questions

What does Penna cost?

Penna is free during the beta. What it costs after that is decided before version 1.0, and what you have written is always your own plain files, whatever happens.

Where are my books saved?

In a folder you choose, as plain files. You can back them up like any other files, and export the book as Markdown if you want to take it somewhere else.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Penna only needs the internet for Google Drive, updates and the dictionaries.

Can I write on several devices?

Yes, with your own Google Drive. The book follows you between computer and phone, and Penna only sees the files it created there itself.

Is Penna available for Mac and iPhone?

macOS is coming. Right now Penna is available for Windows and Android.
