Penna På svenska

A calm place to write your book.

Penna keeps track of chapters, scenes, people and places, so you can write. On your computer and your phone, with your own Google Drive in between if you like.

Download for Windows Android

Free. No account. macOS is coming.

The book as a tree

Parts, chapters and scenes you move with a drag. Word counts at every level.

Notes that find the text

People, places and things. Their names become links in the manuscript, and a series shares them between books.

Review

Repetitions, narrative voice, pacing and what you promised the reader.

Read it as a book

See the text set as pages, and export to standard manuscript, e-book, print PDF or a folder of plain files.

Goals and writing sprints

Daily goals, goals for scene, chapter and book, and an overview of how the book grows.

Notes and pictures

Sticky notes to write and draw on beside the text, and reference pictures for the scene.

In your pocket

The same book on your phone, for a scene on the bus or an idea before you fall asleep. With Google Drive the text follows you between devices, and Penna only sees its own files there.

Your texts are yours

Your books are plain files in a folder you choose. Penna has no accounts, collects nothing and never uses your texts to train AI.
